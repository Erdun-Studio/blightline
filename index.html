Blightline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Who we are

Blightline is a mobile game developed by Bahadır Erdun (Erdun Studio). Contact: erdunstudio@gmail.com

What we collect

We do not ask you for personal information. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

The game has no registration and no account of its own. It can optionally connect to the Google account that is already on your device, through Google Play Games Services — see below.

The game uses these Google services, which process some data of their own:

Google Play Games Services (optional)

Blightline can connect to Google Play Games Services. This is optional. The game asks you once, after your first map, and works fully offline if you decline. You can turn it off at any time from the cloud button on the main menu.

If you connect:

  • Your Play Games profile name and player ID are used to identify you. Your profile name is visible to other players on the Weekly Siege leaderboard.
  • Your Weekly Siege score — the stage you reached, the number of creatures you defeated and your run time — is submitted to a Google Play Games leaderboard and is visible to other players.
  • Your game progress — saves, stars, upgrades and purchased items — is backed up to your own Google account using Play Games Saved Games.

Google stores this data on your behalf. We cannot read your saves. Our own server (see Anonymous gameplay analytics below) never receives them The only data we can see is what any player can see: the public leaderboard.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game offers optional rewarded videos. Blightline never shows an ad on its own — you only ever see one if you press a button that offers a reward for watching, and no part of the game is locked behind them.

If you choose to watch, Google may process your device's advertising ID in order to serve the ad and measure its performance. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Google Play. Your payment details never reach us — we only receive confirmation that a purchase completed, so that the game can unlock the item you bought.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or broken, Blightline sends a small amount of anonymous gameplay data to a server we operate (blightline-api.erdunstudio.com). We use it for one purpose only: to improve the game.

What is sent:

  • which map you started, on which difficulty, and with which heroes;
  • how a run ended — won, lost or abandoned — with the wave you reached, the stars earned, the lives left and how long the run took;
  • a freeze report if the game stops responding: what the game was doing, recent frame timings and your device model. This is how we find crashes and freezes we cannot reproduce ourselves.

What is not sent: your name, your Play Games profile or player ID, your e-mail, your location, your advertising ID, or anything you type. Each installation generates a random number that lets us count players and follow a single run from start to finish; it is not derived from your device or your account, it cannot be linked back to you, and it is not connected to the leaderboard or to purchases.

The data is stored on our own server and is never sold, shared or sent to a third party. Deleting the game deletes the random number, so a fresh install is counted as a new player.

Beyond the Google services above and our own anonymous analytics, there is no tracking and no third-party data sharing.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress — completed maps, upgrades, gems and settings — is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you connect to Google Play Games Services, a copy of that progress is also backed up to your own Google account, so you can restore it on a new device. Items you have purchased are tied to your Google account rather than to your device, so they can be restored if you change phones or reinstall the game.

Children's privacy

Blightline is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Ads are entirely optional; the game never plays one unless you ask for it, and nothing is withheld if you decline.
  • Play Games is entirely optional; the game is fully playable offline.
  • You can turn cloud saves off at any time from the main menu.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the game.
  • For any question about this policy, write to erdunstudio@gmail.com.

Step-by-step instructions, together with what is deleted and what is retained, are on the data deletion page.
