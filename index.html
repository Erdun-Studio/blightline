Blightline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Who we are

Blightline is a mobile game developed by Bahadır Erdun (Erdun Studio). Contact: erdunstudio@gmail.com

What we collect

We do not ask you for personal information. Blightline has no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

However, the game uses two Google services that process some data of their own:

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game offers an optional rewarded video. If you choose to watch it, Google may process your device's advertising ID in order to serve the ad and measure its performance. Watching ads is entirely optional — no part of the game is locked behind them. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled by Google Play. Your payment details never reach us — we only receive confirmation that a purchase completed, so that the game can unlock the item you bought.

Beyond these two services there is no analytics, no tracking and no third-party data sharing.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress — completed maps, upgrades, gems and settings — is stored only on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Items you have purchased are tied to your Google account rather than to your device, so they can be restored if you change phones or reinstall the game.

Children's privacy

Blightline is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • You can decline to watch ads; nothing in the game is withheld.
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the game.
  • For any question about this policy, write to erdunstudio@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date above is changed.
