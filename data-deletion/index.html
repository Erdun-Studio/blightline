Blightline — Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 26 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data related to Blightline, a game published on Google Play by Erdun Studio (Bahadır Erdun).

What data exists

Data Where it is held Retention
Game progress (completed maps, upgrades, gems, settings) Only on your own device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Until you delete it — see step 1 below.
Advertising ID Processed by Google AdMob if you choose to watch a rewarded ad. We never receive it and never store it. Held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. You can reset it at any time — see step 2.
Purchase records Held by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed; payment details never reach us. Kept by Google Play as part of your account's purchase history. Removing it would remove your right to restore purchases.

We operate no server and hold no user account. There is no database on our side containing your data, which means there is nothing for us to retain after you follow the steps below.

How to delete your data

  1. Uninstall Blightline. This immediately and permanently deletes all game progress and settings stored on your device. Nothing survives the uninstall, and nothing is backed up to us.
  2. Reset your advertising ID. On your Android device open Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. This severs the identifier Google AdMob used while you played.
  3. Write to us if you have any further request. Email erdunstudio@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion — Blightline. We reply within 30 days. Because we hold no user data ourselves, our reply will confirm that nothing of yours is stored on our side and will point you to the relevant Google controls for anything held by Google.

What is not deleted

Purchases you made through Google Play remain associated with your Google account so that they can be restored if you reinstall the game or change devices. This record belongs to Google Play, not to us. If you want it removed, contact Google Play support — note that doing so also removes your ability to restore those purchases.
