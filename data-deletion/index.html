Blightline — Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data related to Blightline, a game published on Google Play by Erdun Studio (Bahadır Erdun).

What data exists

Data Where it is held Retention
Game progress (completed maps, upgrades, gems, settings) On your own device. If you connect to Google Play Games Services, a copy is also backed up to your own Google account using Play Games Saved Games. Until you delete it — see steps 1 and 2 below.
Play Games profile name and player ID Held by Google. Used to identify you if you choose to connect; your profile name is visible to other players on the leaderboard. Until you delete your Play Games data — see step 2.
Weekly Siege leaderboard score Held by Google Play Games. Visible to other players. Weekly leaderboards reset every week on their own, and scores are removed when you delete your Play Games data — see step 2.
Advertising ID Processed by Google AdMob if you choose to watch a rewarded ad. We never receive it and never store it. Held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. You can reset it at any time — see step 2.
Purchase records Held by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed; payment details never reach us. Kept by Google Play as part of your account's purchase history. Removing it would remove your right to restore purchases.

We operate no server and hold no user account. There is no database on our side containing your data. Cloud saves and leaderboard scores are held by Google on your behalf — we cannot read your saves, and the only thing we can see is the public leaderboard that every player sees. This means there is nothing for us to retain after you follow the steps below.

How to delete your data

  1. Turn off cloud saves, then uninstall Blightline. In the game, open the cloud button on the main menu and turn cloud saves off — this stops further backups and deletes the stored save. Uninstalling then deletes all game progress and settings stored on your device. Nothing is backed up to us at any point.
  2. Delete your Play Games data for Blightline. Open the Google Play Games app › Settings and delete the Play Games data for this game, or manage it from your Google account settings. This removes your saved games and your leaderboard scores for Blightline.
  3. Reset your advertising ID. On your Android device open Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. This severs the identifier Google AdMob used while you played.
  4. Write to us if you have any further request. Email erdunstudio@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion — Blightline. We reply within 30 days. Because we hold no user data ourselves, our reply will confirm that nothing of yours is stored on our side and will point you to the relevant Google controls for anything held by Google.

What is not deleted

Purchases you made through Google Play remain associated with your Google account so that they can be restored if you reinstall the game or change devices. This record belongs to Google Play, not to us. If you want it removed, contact Google Play support — note that doing so also removes your ability to restore those purchases.
